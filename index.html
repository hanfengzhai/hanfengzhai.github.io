Greetings! I'm Han, and I work in Mechanics and Computation (academic tree). My doctoral research focuses on multiscale materials modeling (defects → microstructures → properties), such as metal strain hardening and homogenization of digital rocks. My industry experience includes work at Mitsubishi Electric Research Labs (AI & energy systems) and Tokyo Electron (computational modeling of 3DI).

I am interested in solving problems in mechanics of materials from atomistic to continuum scales. My research interests are computational mechanics, materials theory, scientific machine learning, and inverse optimization for materials modeling and design, with potential impact on energy, biotechnology, and additive manufacturing. In particular:

  • Theoretical mechanics of solids, e.g., theoretical model for dislocations, mechanistic model for MPEA.
  • Faster and data-driven compute, e.g., graph neural surrogate for polycrystal plasticity, neural ODE for HVAC.
  • Materials design and manufacturing, e.g., optimizations for crystal design, digital design of antimicrobial materials.

I enjoy serving the community and teaching applied mathematics and solid mechanics. I will be co-organizing CompFest 2027 (flyer) and am currently a TA for ME123: Computational Engineering. Previously, I TA'd Mechanics: Elasticity and Inelasticity (see short intro, evaluations) and Finite Element Analysis (see short intro, evaluations). See teaching notes for problem sessions and course reviews. Contact me if you'd like to chat at the 2026 MRS Fall Meeting.

Selected Publications

Link Statistics of Dislocation Network during Strain Hardening

Akhondzadeh1, Zhai1, Jian, Sills, Bertin, Cai*

Journal of the Mechanics and Physics of Solids, 2026

[PDF] [Cite] [Video] [Poster]

Over 100 DDD simulations reveal that inactive slip systems follow single-exponential link length distributions, while active ones evolve into stress-driven double exponentials, explained by a generalized Poisson process.

Stress predictions in polycrystal plasticity using graph neural networks with subgraph training

Zhai

Computational Mechanics, 2025

[PDF] [Cite]

GNNs are used to efficiently predict stress in polycrystal plasticity, achieving over 150x speedup compared to FEM with high accuracy.

Benchmarking inverse optimization algorithms for materials design

Zhai, Hao, Yeo*

APL Materials, 2024

[PDF] [Cite]

We benchmarked different heuristic and machine learning optimization algorithms for designing elemental crystals for targeted properties.

Computational design of antimicrobial active surfaces via automated Bayesian optimization

Zhai & Yeo*

ACS Biomaterials Science & Engineering, 2023

[PDF] [Cite]

A new workflow to digitally design antimicrobial surfaces in the mesoscale using Bayesian optimization and discrete element methods.

Controlling biofilm transport with porous metamaterials designed with Bayesian learning

Zhai & Yeo*

Journal of the Mechanical Behavior of Biomedical Materials, 2023

[PDF] [Cite]

We use Bayesian optimization to design porous materials with enhanced biomass transport and insights into growth mechanisms.
